About

I’m an ANN Changemakers alumna and co-founder of Project Sonali Srijon, working with marginalized women in Rajshahi to build sustainable livelihoods through jute crafts and upcycled plastic products.

Trained in Oriental Art (BFA, MFA) at the University of Rajshahi, my practice blends traditional ink wash and motifs with contemporary community work—using art for empowerment, inclusion, and environmental awareness.
